Blocleaf Cafe

About Blocleaf Cafe

Blocleaf Cafe is a cozy, minimalistic neighborhood cafe tucked away in Malate, Manila. It is renowned for serving locally sourced Philippine coffee in a peaceful, cozy environment ideal for reading, journaling, and light laptop work.

The minimalist wooden aesthetic and abundant natural light make it a favorite quiet hideaway for students looking to escape noisy study halls near campus.
